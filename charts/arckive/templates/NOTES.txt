The Arclight operator is installed.

Three YAMLs are all it takes to start an indexer:
  1. The DSN Secret       (kubectl create secret generic pg-dsn --from-literal=url=postgres://...)
  2. The ABI ConfigMap    (kubectl create configmap usdc-abi --from-file=abi.json)
  3. The Indexer CR       (kubectl apply -f indexer.yaml)

Watch: kubectl get indexers
